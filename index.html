LO5: Two Lenses for Valuation

Investors use two main philosophies to value stocks. They can be used together for a powerful investment strategy.

Fundamental Analysis ("What to buy")

Examines company health (profit, debt, management) to find its "intrinsic value." Assumes market price will eventually reflect true value.

Technical Analysis ("When to buy")

Analyzes price charts and trading volumes to predict future price movements based on patterns and trends.
